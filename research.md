# Research & Academic Projects

### 1. Merging Indigenous Building Science with Modern Civil Engineering
* **Focus:** Low-Carbon, Climate-Resilient Infrastructure Framework
* **Key Findings:** Demonstrated a **55%–65% reduction in embodied carbon** by pairing stabilized earth masonry (CSEBs) with modern structural ring beams and pozzolanic binders compared to standard concrete blocks.

### 2. Plastic Waste-Modified Asphalt Study
* **Focus:** Waste-to-Resource Materials in Pavement Durability
* **Key Findings:** Investigated the utilization of recycled plastic waste as a aggregate substitute in asphalt production to reduce environmental pollution while enhancing pavement performance.
